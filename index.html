AKAN NAME GENERATOR.

Akan names are given according to the day of the week a person is born.Akan names are derived from the Akan people of Ghana and the Ivory Coast. Each day of the week has a corresponding name for males and females. They are regarded as sacred in ancient times and are still widely used today. The Akan people believe that the day of the week a person is born has a significant influence on their character and destiny. Enter your birthday and gender to discover your Akan.
