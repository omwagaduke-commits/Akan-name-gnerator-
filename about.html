About Traditional Names

In Ghanaian culture, children are traditionally given names based on the day of the week they are born. These names represent identity, heritage, and cultural connection.

Male Examples

Sunday - Kwasi
Monday - Kwadwo
Tuesday - Kwabena
Wednesday - Kwaku
Thursday - Yaw
Friday - Kofi
Saturday - Kwame

Female Examples

Sunday - Akosua
Monday - Adwoa
Tuesday - Abenaa
Wednesday - Akua
Thursday - Yaa
Friday - Afua
Saturday - Ama
